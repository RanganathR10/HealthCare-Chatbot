# HealthCare Chatbot
The rapid digital transformation in healthcare has created a growing demand for intelligent 
systems capable of delivering accurate, timely, and personalized medical assistance. This 
project presents an Intelligent Healthcare Chatbot that leverages Large Language Models 
(LLMs) integrated with a domain-specific Healthcare Knowledge Base to provide reliable, 
context-aware, and safe interactions for users. The system combines the natural language 
understanding and reasoning abilities of LLMs with verified medical datasets such as 
disease symptoms, diagnostics, drug information, and treatment guidelines. The chatbot 
supports functionalities including preliminary symptom assessment, health information 
retrieval, medication guidance, appointment assistance, and patient education. To enhance 
safety and compliance, the system incorporates rule-based validation, medical knowledge 
grounding, and fallback mechanisms to avoid misinformation. The hybrid architecture 
ensures high accuracy, explainability, and robustness, while reducing hallucinations 
commonly associated with standalone LLMs. This project demonstrates how advanced AI 
models, coupled with structured healthcare knowledge, can improve accessibility, support 
clinical workflows, and empower users with reliable health insights—ultimately 
contributing to smarter, more efficient, and patient-centric digital healthcare solutions.
